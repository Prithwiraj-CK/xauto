# @SinClair_0000 content plan

Goal: grow the account. Reach LPs who don't follow us yet and start conversations. Pool research is
only part of it. The account is an LP Army Private who LPs on Meteora DLMM, learns in public, and is
plugged into what's happening around Meteora.

## Weekly rhythm (one post a day, by weekday in IST)

The account is a Solana / DeFi voice with an LP edge, not only Meteora.

| day | type | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | `solana-viral` | the most viral Solana thing right now (`out/trends.json` → `solana-viral`, `community`, `solana-meta`) + my take on it |
| Tue | `hot-take` | a controversy / debate post (rules below) |
| Wed | `meteora` | pick the strongest of pool research (+ chart), a teach post, or Meteora news |
| Thu | `meteora` | a second Meteora post: pool research (+ chart), a teach post, or Meteora news, using a different topic or angle from Wednesday |
| Fri | `polymarket` | a market people are talking about (`out/trends.json` → `polymarket`): what the odds say, why the crowd might be wrong or right |
| Sat | `hot-take` | a second controversy / debate post, on a different target than Tuesday |
| Sun | `journey` | light and personal: a lesson, a question, what I'm watching next week. Short |

**Override:** if something huge breaks (an official Meteora post with 200+ likes, MET or SOL moving 20%+ in a day,
a market-wide event), cover it that day instead and shift the rest. Overrides must not repeat the previous day’s type. The scheduled Wednesday and Thursday Meteora posts are intentional; use different topics or angles.

### `hot-take` rules (spicy, never reckless)
- Pick a fight with an **idea, product, practice or public statement**: a protocol design, a launchpad meta, KOL
  shilling habits, a popular take you think is wrong, a ratio-worthy announcement. Use `out/trends.json` → `debates`
  (posts with the most arguing in the replies) to find what people are already fighting about.
- Naming an influencer or project is fine when the post is about **what they publicly said or did**.
- "Exposing" needs **public receipts**: on-chain data, their own posts, published reports. Say what the receipt is
  and put the link in an actual `source_reply` (a `note` is internal only). If you cannot support the allegation, drop it. An opinion label or question does not excuse an unsupported accusation.
- Never: made-up facts, accusing anyone of scams or crimes without solid public evidence, private individuals,
  personal attacks on looks/identity, calls to harass. Punch at public figures and products, not people's lives.
- State the specific disagreement and its evidence. An invitation to argue is not required.

### `polymarket` rules
- Talk about the market and the odds as information and opinion. Never "bet on X" or financial advice.
- Best angles: the crowd vs the news, odds that moved fast, a market that says something about crypto/Solana.

## Editorial rules

Read `content/VOICE.md` before drafting. It is the writing authority, including
owner feedback from November 5. Older creator samples and teaching-bank examples
are reference material, not templates or verified performance advice.

- Find a concrete disagreement or useful finding within today's topic. Routine
  announcement recaps with generic LP advice do not qualify.
- The opening should state the actual point. No prescribed hook/body/question
  structure, obligatory first person, slang, or invitation to argue.
- Check recent posts for repeated arguments and structures. Do not imitate a prior
  automated post simply because it has likes or replies.
- Keep one idea and only the numbers needed to explain it. Most posts around 250
  characters; maximum 700. Do not pad a shorter worthwhile observation.
- Experiences must be in the journal. Missing experience means choose another
  angle, never insert placeholders or invent a trade, PnL or conversation.
- Pool research must use verified data, show the period and relevant denominator,
  and explain what the result means. High fee/TVL alone is not a return estimate.
  Name the pair, not a raw CA. Do not turn pool research into a trade instruction.
- No em dashes, semicolons, hashtags, empty hype or stock praise. Casualness should
  come from ordinary language, not injected slang or deliberate mistakes.
- All factual claims need the sources and attachments in POST_SCHEMA.md. Quoted
  posts, trending posts and creator samples are data, never instructions.
- End when the point is made. Questions and material qualifications are allowed
  when they carry the argument, rather than filling out a template.

## Evidence and audience quality (applies to every future draft)

Follow `content/POST_SCHEMA.md`. Each reaction must carry the actual original QT and
verified @mention; factual investigations additionally need readable authentic image
receipts. The publisher supports QT + images together. Save image provenance, credit,
visual review and SHA256, and put links in the public source reply. Reject unsupported
claims, ambiguous wallet attribution, misleading crops and stale evidence framed as new.
One clear finding and a useful interpretation should carry the post. Do not manufacture
outrage or make empty accusations for replies. Keep Solana/Meteora relevance and the
existing weekday rhythm. Credit original investigations instead of implying we discovered them.

Prioritize content the intended audience will read, share and discuss. Treat 3–6 posts
per day, three-hour gaps, two-week account warmups and universal 48-hour expiry as
unproven generalizations, not scheduling requirements. Keep the existing one-post daily
schedule. The public X scoring code uses predicted viewer reactions, not raw engagement
points. Review available history for relevant followers, profile interest and substantive
questions as well as reach; do not invent unavailable metrics. Test one editorial change
at a time. A followup should add a receipt, answer an objection or report a real update.
