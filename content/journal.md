# LP journal (the human writes this, the bot reads it)

Add a line or two whenever something happens: a position you opened or closed, a win, a loss, a mistake,
something you noticed, a chat with another LP, where you're LPing from. Newest at the top.
The daily post uses these for its first-person angle. It never invents personal details.
Edit on github.com (pencil icon) or ask Claude to add a line.
